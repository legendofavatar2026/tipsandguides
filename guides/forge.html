Forge Guide 🔨

Updated July 2026

Introduction

The Forge system allows you to add powerful bonus stats to your equipment. Choosing the right Forge rolls can significantly improve your overall damage, especially for Double Combo builds.

This guide covers the recommended Forge stat priorities for both early progression and endgame optimization.

Early Progression (Tier D → Tier S)

During the early stages of the game, it is recommended to prioritize Final Attack over Bonus Boss Damage.

Most of your progression comes from clearing normal stages rather than defeating bosses, making Final Attack the better overall choice for pushing stages and farming efficiently.

Recommended Setup

4 Forge Slots

  • Final Double Combo Damage ×3
  • Final Attack ×1

5 Forge Slots

  • Final Double Combo Damage ×4
  • Final Attack ×1

Continue using this setup until you reach approximately S Tier equipment.

Mid Progression (SS Tier)

After reaching SS Tier equipment, boss encounters become a larger part of your progression. At this stage, Final Attack begins to lose value compared to Bonus Boss Damage.

Start replacing your Final Attack Forge roll with Bonus Boss Damage.

Recommended Setup

  • Final Double Combo Damage ×4
  • Bonus Boss Damage ×1

This serves as a smooth transition into the endgame while maintaining excellent overall damage.

Endgame (SSS Tier / 6 Forge Slots)

Once you have unlocked all six Forge slots and reached late-game progression, Bonus Boss Damage becomes much more valuable.

Recommended Setup (Balanced)

  • Final Double Combo Damage ×3
  • Bonus Boss Damage ×3

This is the recommended setup for most players because it provides the best balance between Final Double Combo Damage and Bonus Boss Damage.

Alternative Setup

  • Final Double Combo Damage ×4
  • Bonus Boss Damage ×2

If your build already has plenty of Bonus Boss Damage from other sources, this setup may provide slightly better results depending on your overall stat distribution.

Why This Works

Final Double Combo Damage has numerous sources throughout the game, totaling roughly 556%. Each Forge roll contributes approximately 60%, making the first few rolls extremely efficient.

As you gain more Final Double Combo Damage from other systems, additional Forge rolls begin to experience diminishing returns.

Meanwhile, Bonus Boss Damage has very few competing sources outside of Forge, allowing each roll to retain much higher value throughout the late game.

Because of this, transitioning from Final Attack to Bonus Boss Damage as your equipment improves results in higher overall damage efficiency.

Forge Progression Summary

Equipment Tier Recommended Forge Setup
D → S Tier Final Double Combo Damage ×3–4 + Final Attack ×1
SS Tier Final Double Combo Damage ×4 + Bonus Boss Damage ×1
SSS Tier (6 Slots) Final Double Combo Damage ×3 + Bonus Boss Damage ×3 (Recommended)
Alternative Endgame Final Double Combo Damage ×4 + Bonus Boss Damage ×2

Quick Summary

  • Prioritize Final Attack while progressing through D Tier to S Tier.
  • Once you reach SS Tier, begin replacing Final Attack with Bonus Boss Damage.
  • For endgame, use either 3× Final Double Combo Damage + 3× Bonus Boss Damage (recommended) or 4× Final Double Combo Damage + 2× Bonus Boss Damage.
  • Review your Forge rolls periodically as future updates may introduce new damage sources and change stat priorities.

Build Variations

The Forge progression shown in this guide uses Final Double Combo Damage as the primary example.

If you are playing a different build, simply replace Final Double Combo Damage with the corresponding Forge stat while following the same progression and stat ratios.

  • Double Combo Build → Final Double Combo Damage
  • Counter Build → Final Counter Damage
  • Skill Build → Final Skill Critical Damage

The recommendations for Final Attack during early progression and Bonus Boss Damage during mid and late progression remain the same regardless of your build.
