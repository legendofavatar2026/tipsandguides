Forge Guide 🔨

Updated July 2026

Introduction

The Forge system allows you to add powerful bonus stats to your equipment. Choosing the right Forge rolls can significantly improve your overall damage, especially for Double Combo builds.

This guide covers the recommended Forge stat priorities for both early progression and endgame optimization.

Early Progression

During the early game, it is recommended to prioritize Final Attack over Bonus Boss Damage.

Most of your progression comes from clearing normal stages rather than fighting bosses, making Final Attack the better overall choice for pushing stages and farming efficiently.

Recommended Setup

4 Forge Slots

  • Final Double Combo Damage ×3
  • Final Attack ×1

5 Forge Slots

  • Final Double Combo Damage ×4
  • Final Attack ×1

Endgame Setup (6 Forge Slots)

Once all six Forge slots are unlocked and your focus shifts toward maximizing boss damage, transition away from Final Attack and prioritize Bonus Boss Damage instead.

Recommended Setups

Balanced Setup (Recommended)

  • Final Double Combo Damage ×3
  • Bonus Boss Damage ×3

This setup provides the most balanced distribution between Final Double Combo Damage and Bonus Boss Damage, making it the preferred choice for most endgame players.

Alternative Setup

  • Final Double Combo Damage ×4
  • Bonus Boss Damage ×2

This setup places slightly more emphasis on Final Double Combo Damage and may perform better depending on your existing stats and available sources of Bonus Boss Damage.

Why This Works

Final Double Combo Damage has numerous sources throughout the game, totaling roughly 556%. Each Forge roll contributes approximately 60%, making the first few rolls extremely efficient.

As additional sources of Final Double Combo Damage are acquired, each extra Forge roll experiences diminishing returns.

Meanwhile, Bonus Boss Damage is primarily obtained through Forge, meaning each roll retains a much higher effective value compared to stats with many competing sources.

Because of this interaction, mixing the two stats generally produces higher overall damage than investing entirely into a single stat.

Which Setup Should You Choose?

  • Early Progression (4 Slots): 3× Final Double Combo Damage + 1× Final Attack
  • Early Progression (5 Slots): 4× Final Double Combo Damage + 1× Final Attack
  • Endgame (6 Slots): 3× Final Double Combo Damage + 3× Bonus Boss Damage (Recommended)
  • Alternative Endgame: 4× Final Double Combo Damage + 2× Bonus Boss Damage

Quick Summary

  • Prioritize Final Attack during early progression.
  • Switch to Bonus Boss Damage once SS Tier is unlocked.
  • The recommended endgame setup is 3× Final Double Combo Damage + 3× Bonus Boss Damage.
  • A 4:2 ratio is also a viable alternative depending on your overall stat distribution.
  • Re-evaluate your Forge rolls as new systems and stats are introduced in future updates.
