Gear Affix Guide ⚙️

Updated July 2026

Introduction

Gear Affixes become one of the most important progression systems as you obtain higher rarity equipment. Choosing the right affixes at each stage of the game will save you a large amount of resources and help maximize your damage.

This guide explains what affixes to prioritize during the Early, Mid, and Late Game.

🌱 Early Game (Legendary and Below)

Before reaching Mythic equipment, your main goal is simply obtaining the highest possible base stats.

  • ✅ ATK
  • ✅ DEF
  • ✅ HP

At this stage, the affix itself is not important. Focus only on replacing gear with better overall stats.

Single-affix equipment is available up to the Legendary tier.

⚔️ Mid Early Game (Mythic Gear)

Mythic equipment introduces 2 Affix Slots.

You should still prioritize gear with the highest ATK, DEF, and HP, but now you can begin building useful affixes.

Recommended Affix

  • ⭐ Double Combo Chance

Double Combo Chance is especially valuable if:

  • You haven't obtained Hanami.
  • Your Mythic Avatar isn't Elysia or Alice.
  • Your build lacks enough Double Combo Chance.

Rolling Double Combo Chance on multiple gear pieces is one of the easiest ways to close the gap until stronger sources become available.

🔥 Mid Game (Eternity Gear)

Once you unlock Eternity Gear, it becomes time to slowly move away from stacking Double Combo Chance.

By this point, you'll have access to many additional sources of Double Combo Chance, especially through the Emblem System.

Recommended Affixes

  • Critical Damage
  • Critical Damage + Double Combo Chance (if still lacking Double Combo)

Critical Damage provides much better long-term scaling as your account progresses.

👑 Late Game (Eternity I Gear)

Eternity I equipment introduces a third Affix Slot, allowing Pierce-related stats to appear.

Since Pierce mechanics are still being researched by the community, there isn't enough information yet to determine the optimal setup.

For now, continue using the same strategy as Eternity Gear by prioritizing Critical Damage while evaluating useful Pierce rolls when available.

This guide will be updated once additional testing has been completed.

❓ Frequently Asked Question

Should I lock an affix using the dropdown filter?

Generally, no.

It is usually better to leave the checkbox unchecked.

Eventually you'll need multiple equipment presets for:

  • Double Combo Build
  • Counter Build
  • Skill Critical Damage Build

Keeping the filter unlocked allows you to naturally obtain useful affixes for different builds instead of spending unnecessary Orbs trying to force a specific affix.

Whenever you roll a useful affix, simply save that equipment into another Gear Preset for future content such as:

  • World Boss
  • Infinite Tower
  • Arena
  • Guild War

💡 Final Tips

  • Early Game → Prioritize Base Stats.
  • Mythic Gear → Build Double Combo Chance.
  • Eternity Gear → Transition into Critical Damage.
  • Eternity I → Continue Critical Damage while evaluating Pierce.
  • Keep multiple gear presets instead of rerolling everything into one build.
