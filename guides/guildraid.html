Guild Raid — TL;DR

  • ⚔️ Cooperative PvE: Guild members work together to defeat a powerful raid boss.
  • 📅 Weekly Content: Guild Raid follows the same schedule, server group, and season system as Guild War.
  • 📈 Contribution Points: Daily activities such as stage clears and summons generate Contribution Points, which provide guild-wide ATK, DEF, and HP bonuses.
  • 🐉 Three Boss Parts: The raid boss has three parts, each designed around a different build: Head → Combo, Tail → Skill, Soulstone → Counter.
  • 👥 Party System: Each boss part has 10 parties, with 4 player slots per party.
  • ✨ Guild Raid Skills: When setting up a party, you can equip up to 3 Guild Raid Skills.
  • 🛡️ Formations Matter: Vanguard and Rearguard positioning is important because some boss attacks target specific rows.
  • 🏆 No Entry Cost: You can freely change your setup and challenge the raid without additional entry costs or limits.
  • 📊 Records: Your highest score becomes your Personal Record. Party members' highest scores combine into the Party Record, while all Party Records combine into the Guild Raid Cumulative Score.
  • 🎁 Season Rewards: Rewards include Top Specialist, Best Raider, Best Conquest Guild, and Contribution Ranking rewards.
