Find Divine Weapon combinations by selecting two or more weapons. You can select up to four weapons to narrow down the results.

ℹ️ How It Works

  • Select at least 2 weapons to begin searching.
  • Selecting a third or fourth weapon further narrows the results.
  • Only combinations containing all selected weapons are displayed.
  • The result shows the corresponding stat, available amount values, and all weapons in that combination.
  • Weapon selections cannot be duplicated.
  • No combination data is displayed until you make a selection.

📌 Notes

  • The finder uses the Divine Weapon combination database.
  • Some combinations may contain fewer than four weapons.
  • Selecting four weapons only returns combinations containing all four selected weapons.
  • Results are grouped together when the same weapon combination and stat have multiple amount values.
