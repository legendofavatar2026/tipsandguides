⛵ Sailing & Captain's Cabin Guide

Everything you need to know about Sailing, Plunder, Mercenaries, Navigation Tools, Synergy and Blessings.

⚓ Sailing — TL;DR

  • 🚢 Sailing: Dispatch ships to collect Navigation Tools, Essence of Navigation and Hidden Rewards.
  • 🕒 Daily Voyages: You can dispatch up to 7 ships per day, with a maximum of 3 ships sailing simultaneously.
  • 🌊 Change Route: Improve your destination's sea area grade before setting sail. Higher grades offer better rewards.
  • ⚔️ Plunder: Attack other captains' ships to steal one Hidden Reward. You have 20 attempts per day.
  • 🤝 Mercenary: Hire guild members to help defend your ship or fight alongside you during Plunder.
  • 🧭 Navigation Tools: Activate and upgrade five different tools to increase your character's stats.
  • ✨ Synergy: All five Navigation Tools must be activated. Your lowest tool grade and Maintenance level determine your Synergy progression.
  • 📜 Blessings: Equip Blessings for additional stats, complete your Collection, and synthesize duplicates into higher-grade Blessings.
  • 🗺️ Treasure Map Fragments: Obtain them from Hidden Rewards and exchange them for Sealed Blessings.
🔓 Unlock Requirements

Server Day 7 or later and clear Stage 300-10.

Location: Domain → Sailing / Captain's Cabin

🚢 Sailing — Dispatch Your Ships

Sailing is the main way to obtain materials for your Captain's Cabin. Send your ships on voyages to collect Navigation Tools, Essence of Navigation and Hidden Rewards.

🕒 Port Opening Schedule

Port Opening (EST) Duration
15:00 – 16:00 1 Hour
22:00 – 23:00 1 Hour
  • You can set sail 7 times per day.
  • You can dispatch up to 3 ships simultaneously.
  • Each voyage takes approximately 15–20 minutes.
  • Your daily sailing attempts reset at 19:00 EST.
  • Ships already sailing will complete their voyages even after the port closes.
💡 Important:

You can divide your seven daily voyages between the two port openings. You do not have to use all your attempts during the first opening.

Ships are protected from Plunder for approximately 5 minutes after departure, until they arrive at their sea area.

🌊 Change Route — Aim for Higher Grades

Before setting sail, you can change your route to attempt to reach a higher-grade sea area. Higher sea area grades provide better potential rewards.

Sea Area Grades:

Uncommon → Rare → Heroic → Legendary

  • Each ship has a limited number of route changes.
  • Changing your route will never lower your current sea area grade.
  • Once you reach Legendary, you can no longer change the route.

💡 Tip: Use your available route changes before dispatching. Try to improve your sea area grade rather than immediately settling for a lower-grade destination.

🎁 Understanding Sailing Rewards

Reward Type Description Can Be Plundered?
Sailing Rewards Navigation Tools, Essence of Navigation and other materials. No
Hidden Rewards Treasure discovered when your ship arrives at its sea area. Yes
Mercenary Bonus Additional rewards for hiring a mercenary when setting sail. No
📌 Remember:

The contents of Hidden Rewards remain concealed during the voyage. You can only see their grades until your ship returns. Any Hidden Rewards that were not plundered will be received when the voyage ends.

⚔️ Plunder — Steal Hidden Rewards

Plunder allows you to attack other captains' ships and take one of their Hidden Rewards if you win.

📌 Plunder Mechanics

  • Available for 2 hours after each port opening.
  • Maximum of 20 Plunder attempts per day.
  • Attempts reset daily at 19:00 EST.
  • Each ship can be plundered up to 3 times.
  • Battles are 2 vs. 2: You and your mercenary against the opposing captain and mercenary.
  • If you lose, you do not lose any of your own rewards.
  • You can use Sweep to consume an attempt and receive Plunder rewards without fighting.

🎯 Choosing Your Plunder Targets

Not every ship is worth attacking. Your target should depend on the rewards you currently need and your chances of winning.

The following recommendations are based on player observations and discussions, not official drop-rate information.

Ship Grade Community Observation Suggested Objective
Green Generally more manageable targets, but players reported fewer desirable lower-grade anchors. Farm more manageable targets and look for Map Fragments.
Blue Reportedly better for finding grey and green anchors. Complete lower-grade Blessing Collection entries.
Purple / Legendary Can contain higher-grade rewards, but may have stronger defenders. Attempt higher-grade rewards when the opponent is manageable.

📜 What Should You Farm?

Approach A — Complete Your Blessing Collection

  • Prioritize targets that can provide missing grey and green anchors.
  • Players reported that Blue ships are useful for farming these lower-grade anchors.
  • Complete missing Blessings to obtain their Collection stats.
  • Move toward higher-grade Blessings as your collection progresses.

Approach B — Farm Treasure Map Fragments

  • Green ships were suggested as more manageable targets.
  • Players reported that they can provide Map Fragments, although the amount may be lower.
  • Exchange your Map Fragments for Sealed Blessings at the Captain's Cabin Exchange.
💡 Community Recommendation:

Choose your Plunder targets based on your current progression. If you need Collection entries, focus on the relevant lower-grade rewards. If you need Sealed Blessings, consider farming Treasure Map Fragments.

Do not automatically attack the highest-grade ship. A difficult opponent may not be worth the additional effort or Diamond spending.

💎 Should You Spend Diamonds on Additional Plunders?

Players have discussed spending up to 10,000 Diamonds on additional Plunder attempts. However, whether this is worthwhile depends on your target, available rewards and battle success rate.

  • Prioritize your free daily attempts first.
  • Consider the strength of the defending captain before attacking.
  • Hiring a mercenary may add to your spending.
  • Avoid repeatedly refreshing targets without a clear objective.

💡 Tip: One player suggested limiting individual attack spending to around 3,000–5,000. This is a personal recommendation, not an established optimal spending limit.

🔎 Useful Plunder Features

  • Most Wanted: Register a captain who plundered your ship and go after them when they set sail again.
  • Pirate's Message: Leave a message for another captain after a successful Plunder. Messages have a 30-character limit.
  • Plunder Log: Review attacks against your ship, watch battle replays and share them in chat.

🤝 Mercenary — Fight Alongside Guild Members

You can hire one guild member as a mercenary when setting sail or attacking another captain through Plunder.

🛡️ Mercenary Roles

  • When Setting Sail: Your mercenary helps defend your ship when another player attempts to plunder it. You also receive a Mercenary Bonus.
  • When Plundering: Your mercenary fights alongside you, helping you defeat the opposing captain.

📌 Daily Limits

  • You can hire mercenaries 20 times per day.
  • The limit is shared between Sailing and Plunder.
  • The same guild member can be hired up to 3 times per day.
  • If your guild has 10 or fewer members, or you are not in a guild, helper characters may also appear.

🎁 Mercenary Spoils

Whenever another guild member hires you as a mercenary, you accumulate Mercenary Spoils.

  • Claim Mercenary Spoils from the Guild screen.
  • You can claim up to 30 per day.
  • Unclaimed Mercenary Spoils disappear at 19:00 EST.
💡 Tip:

Coordinate with your guild members. Hiring each other can help with Sailing defense, Plunder battles and Mercenary Spoil collection.

Remember to claim your Mercenary Spoils before the daily reset.

🧭 Captain's Cabin — Navigation Tools

The Captain's Cabin is where you use materials obtained through Sailing to improve your character's stats.

There are five different Navigation Tools, each providing additional stats when activated.

🧰 The Five Navigation Tools

  • Compass
  • Spyglass
  • Hourglass
  • Nautical Map
  • Logbook

⭐ Navigation Tool Grades

Common → Uncommon → Rare → Heroic → Legendary → Mythic

  • Obtaining a tool for the first time activates it and increases your stats.
  • When you obtain a higher grade, you can switch to it using a single button.
  • Obtaining the same or a lower grade again automatically converts it into that tool's Essence of Navigation.
💡 Important:

Duplicate Navigation Tools are not wasted. They are converted into Essence of Navigation, which is used for Maintenance.

🔧 Maintenance — Enhance Your Tools

Use Essence of Navigation to maintain your Navigation Tools and further increase their stats.

  • Maintenance increases the stats of your Navigation Tools.
  • When a tool reaches maximum Maintenance, additional Essence of Navigation can be exchanged for Sealed Blessings.
  • The exchange is available through the Essence of Navigation Exchange.

Tip: Keep upgrading your tools instead of treating duplicate materials as useless drops.

✨ Synergy — Why Balanced Upgrades Matter

⚠️ Important Progression Mechanic

Synergy only activates after all five Navigation Tools have been activated.

Your lowest Navigation Tool grade and lowest Maintenance level determine your Synergy progression.

⭐ Grade Synergy

Grade Synergy provides additional stats based on the lowest-grade Navigation Tool among your five tools. It also determines how many Blessing slots are available per tool.

Lowest Tool Grade Blessing Slots Per Tool
Common / Uncommon 1
Rare 2
Heroic 3
Legendary 4
Mythic 5

🔧 Maintenance Synergy

Maintenance Synergy provides additional stats whenever the lowest Maintenance level among your five Navigation Tools reaches certain milestones.

💡 Recommended Strategy: Upgrade Evenly

Avoid focusing all your resources on just one Navigation Tool. Your Synergy progression is determined by your weakest tool.

If four tools are already at a higher grade but one is still behind, improving the weaker tool is the better way to reach your next Synergy milestone.

📜 Blessings — Additional Stats & Collection

Blessings are equipped on Navigation Tools to provide additional character stats.

📌 Blessing Mechanics

  • There are 25 Blessings in total.
  • Each Navigation Tool has its own set of 5 Blessing types.
  • You can equip multiple Blessings of the same type.
  • Equipping more Blessings of the same type activates higher set-effect tiers.
  • Use Equip Set to equip Blessings of the same type all at once.
  • Obtaining a Blessing for the first time registers it in your Blessing Collection and grants Collection stats.
💡 Tip:

Do not focus only on the grade of your equipped Blessings. Completing new entries in your Blessing Collection also provides additional stats.

🔮 How to Obtain Blessings

1. Blessing Refinement

Use a Sealed Blessing to obtain a Blessing. A special refinement animation plays when you obtain a Heroic-grade or higher Blessing.

2. Blessing Synthesis

Combine 3 Blessings of the same grade and Navigation Tool type to attempt to obtain one Blessing of the next higher grade.

Examples:

  • 3 Common Compass Blessings → Attempt for 1 Uncommon Compass Blessing.
  • 3 Rare Logbook Blessings → Attempt for 1 Heroic Logbook Blessing.
⚠️ Protect Your Valuable Blessings
  • Add important Blessings to Favorites to prevent them from being used as synthesis materials.
  • Blessings equipped in a Preset are also excluded from synthesis materials.

3. Treasure Map Fragments

Treasure Map Fragments obtained from Hidden Rewards can be exchanged for Sealed Blessings at the Captain's Cabin Exchange.

This is another reason to consider farming Hidden Rewards through Plunder.

📂 Presets

You can save up to 10 presets containing your activated Navigation Tools and equipped Blessings.

Use presets to save different setups without having to manually rebuild your Navigation Tool and Blessing configurations.

💡 Player-Shared Tips & Observations

The following tips are based on player discussions and personal experiences. They are not official drop-rate guarantees.

1. Don't Automatically Attack Legendary Ships

Legendary ships may contain higher-grade rewards, but their defenders can also be significantly stronger.

One player reported difficulty plundering efficiently because many Legendary ships were defended by highly progressed players.

Takeaway: Check the opponent's strength before committing additional resources. A lower-grade ship that you can defeat consistently may be a better target.

2. Check the Second Port Opening

One player observed fewer active Sailing ships during the first port opening, while more players appeared during the second.

This may depend on server activity and the number of active players within your server group.

Takeaway: If your server has low Sailing activity during the first opening, check the second opening for more available targets.

3. Don't Neglect Your Logbook

One player shared a progression example after obtaining a Legendary Logbook, reporting a 21.4 Oc power increase, approximately 1.56%.

This is an individual player result, not a guaranteed increase for every account.

Takeaway: Do not neglect any of the five Navigation Tools. Even one tool upgrade can contribute meaningfully to your overall progression.

4. Be Careful With Diamond Refresh Costs

Players raised concerns about the cost of refreshing Plunder targets, particularly when trying to farm specific Hidden Rewards.

One player suggested reducing the 100-Diamond refresh cost to 20 Diamonds. This was a player suggestion, not an implemented change.

Takeaway: Set a spending limit before refreshing. Avoid spending repeatedly without a clear farming objective.

📅 Recommended Daily Routine

Use this checklist to keep your Sailing progression organized.

🚢 During Port Opening

  • Dispatch ships and use available route changes to aim for higher sea area grades.
  • Hire guild mercenaries when appropriate.
  • Check Plunder targets and prioritize the rewards you currently need.
  • Use free Plunder attempts before considering additional Diamond spending.

🎁 After Your Ships Return

  • Claim your Sailing and Hidden Rewards.
  • Activate any newly obtained Navigation Tool grades.
  • Use duplicate Navigation Tools for Maintenance.
  • Check your lowest Navigation Tool grade and Maintenance level.
  • Refine, equip or synthesize Blessings as needed.
  • Claim your Mercenary Spoils before the daily reset.

🏆 Final Recommendations

If you are just starting Sailing, prioritize the following:

  • 1. Activate all five Navigation Tools. You need all five activated for Synergy to work.
  • 2. Upgrade their grades evenly. Your lowest-grade tool determines your Grade Synergy.
  • 3. Work on Maintenance Synergy. Improve your weakest tool instead of investing everything into one.
  • 4. Complete your Blessing Collection. First-time Blessing entries provide additional Collection stats.
  • 5. Use Plunder strategically. Choose targets based on whether you need Blessing Collection entries or Treasure Map Fragments.
  • 6. Avoid unnecessary Diamond spending. Prioritize free attempts and consider the strength of your target before spending more.
⚓ The Main Takeaway

Sailing is a long-term progression system. Consistent rewards, balanced Navigation Tool upgrades and smart Blessing management are more important than simply chasing the highest-grade ships.
